Privacy policy

Last updated: June 2026

Kkotgil is currently in private beta. This public page describes our intended privacy approach for the product pilot.

  • We collect only the information needed to operate saved routes, place reports, and basic account features.
  • Location-related features are designed to support local discovery and are not sold to third parties.
  • User-submitted reports may be reviewed to improve route quality and bloom-status accuracy.
  • Operational logs may be used to secure the service, fix bugs, and understand reliability during the pilot.

For privacy questions, contact hello@studionani.kr.
